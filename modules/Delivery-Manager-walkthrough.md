# Delivery Manager Walkthrough

## Summary

As Delivery Manager, use the local toolkit to gather Jira project data and
prepare customer and senior-leadership weekly status report drafts. Review
the facts, edit the wording, set delivery health, and decide whether each
item is appropriate for its audience before sharing. Jira credentials and
project data should remain local.

## Quiz

1. Who has final control over the report wording and health rating?
2. What should happen when content cannot be safely classified for a customer
   report?
3. Does the toolkit send the report automatically?

### Answers

1. The Delivery Manager.
2. Present it for Delivery Manager review rather than including it
   automatically.
3. No. Reports are drafts for review and are not automatically distributed.
