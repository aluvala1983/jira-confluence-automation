# Project Overview Walkthrough

## Summary

This project is a local Jira status-reporting toolkit. Its goal is to gather
Jira data for a development project and prepare editable weekly report drafts
for customer and internal leadership audiences. The Delivery Manager reviews
the content and controls final wording and sensitive information before
sharing.

## Quiz

1. Who reviews and approves the report drafts before sharing?
2. Why are separate customer and leadership drafts useful?
3. Should sensitive internal commentary be included in a customer-facing draft?

### Answers

1. The Delivery Manager.
2. Each audience needs an appropriate level of detail.
3. No. Sensitive content should be excluded or flagged for review.
