# Customer Walkthrough

## Summary

The customer-facing weekly report is an audience-appropriate view of project
progress. It can summarize completed work, open work, upcoming plans, and
relevant delivery risks. Internal commentary, individual staffing details,
and risks designated sensitive must not appear in this draft.

## Quiz

1. Which kinds of information may be summarized in a customer report?
2. Should internal commentary or individual staffing details be included?
3. Who reviews the draft before it is shared?

### Answers

1. Customer-appropriate completed work, open work, upcoming plans, and
   relevant risks.
2. No.
3. The Delivery Manager.
