# Senior Leadership Walkthrough

## Summary

The senior-leadership weekly report provides an internal view of project
delivery. It can highlight progress, open work, next week's plan, blockers
and risks, and overall delivery health. The Delivery Manager reviews the
draft and controls its final wording before sharing.

## Quiz

1. What is the leadership report intended to summarize?
2. Who reviews and finalizes its wording?
3. Is this report the same audience-specific draft as the customer report?

### Answers

1. Project progress, open work, upcoming plans, blockers and risks, and
   delivery health.
2. The Delivery Manager.
3. No. It is a separate internal draft tailored to senior leadership.
